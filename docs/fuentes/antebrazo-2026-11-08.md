### Radio (`radio`)

*Radius* · TA2 n.º 1210

- **Descripción.** Hueso largo del antebrazo, lateral y paralelo a la ulna. Gira alrededor de la ulna y así produce el movimiento de las articulaciones radioulnares proximal y distal.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The radius is a long bone in the forearm. It lies laterally and parallel to ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The radius pivots around the ulna to produce movement at the proximal and distal radio-ulnar joints.”
- **Función.** Al girar alrededor de la ulna mueve el antebrazo en las articulaciones radioulnares, y da inserción al bíceps braquial y al pronador redondo.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The radius pivots around the ulna to produce movement at the proximal and distal radio-ulnar joints.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “serves as the place of attachment of the biceps brachii muscle”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “small roughening for the attachment of the pronator teres muscle”
- **Partes.** Proximal: cabeza (un disco de superficie articular cóncava, más gruesa por dentro), cuello y tuberosidad del radio. Diáfisis triangular, con tres bordes y tres caras, que se ensancha hacia abajo. Distal: proceso estiloides en el lado lateral y escotadura ulnar en el medial.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “A disk shaped structure, with a concave articulating surface. It is thicker medially”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “A narrow area of bone, which lies between the radial head and radial tuberosity”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The radial shaft expands in diameter as it moves distally. Much like the ulna, it is…”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The lateral side projects distally as the styloid process”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “there is a concavity, called the ulnar notch”
- **Articulaciones.** Codo: la cabeza del radio con el capítulo del húmero. Radioulnar proximal: la cabeza del radio con la escotadura radial de la ulna. Muñeca: el extremo distal con los huesos del carpo (escafoides y semilunar). Radioulnar distal: la escotadura ulnar con la cabeza de la ulna.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “Partly formed by an articulation between the head of the radius, and the capitulum of the…”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “An articulation between the radial head, and the radial notch of the ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “An articulation between the distal end of the radius and the carpal bones”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “An articulation between the ulnar notch and the head of the ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “two facets, for articulation with the scaphoid and lunate carpal bones”
- **Inserciones musculares.** Tuberosidad del radio: bíceps braquial. Mitad de la cara lateral de la diáfisis: pronador redondo. Cara anterior: flexor largo del pulgar y pronador cuadrado. Diáfisis: cabeza radial del flexor superficial de los dedos.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “serves as the place of attachment of the biceps brachii muscle”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “In the middle of the lateral surface, there is a small roughening for the attachment of…”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “Originates from the anterior surface of the radius and surrounding interosseous membrane”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “attaches to the anterior surface of the radius”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “arise from the medial epicondyle of the humerus and the shaft of the radius”
- **Relación.** El radio y la ulna están unidos por la membrana interósea.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The radius and the ulna are attached by the interosseous membrane”

### Ulna (`ulna`)

*Ulna* · TA2 n.º 1230

- **Descripción.** Hueso largo del antebrazo, medial y paralelo al radio. Es el hueso estable: el radio gira a su alrededor para producir el movimiento.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The ulna is a long bone in the forearm. It lies medially and parallel to the…”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The ulna acts as the stabilising bone, with the radius pivoting to produce movement.”
- **Función.** Forma con el húmero la articulación del codo y da inserción a músculos como el tríceps braquial, el braquial y el pronador cuadrado.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “Proximally, the ulna articulates with the humerus at the elbow joint.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The triceps brachii muscle attaches to its superior surface.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “It is where the brachialis muscle attaches.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “site of attachment for the pronator quadratus muscle distally”
- **Partes.** Proximal: olécranon (se palpa como la «punta» del codo), proceso coronoides, escotadura troclear (formada por los dos anteriores), escotadura radial y tuberosidad de la ulna. Diáfisis triangular, con tres bordes y tres caras, que se estrecha hacia abajo; su borde posterior se palpa a lo largo de todo el antebrazo. Distal: cabeza redondeada y proceso estiloides de la ulna.

  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “formed by the olecranon and coronoid process”

  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The ulnar shaft is triangular in shape, with three borders and three surfaces. As it moves…”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “palpable along the entire length of the forearm posteriorly”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “terminating in a rounded head, with distal projection”
- **Articulaciones.** Codo: la escotadura troclear con la tróclea del húmero. Radioulnar proximal: la escotadura radial con la cabeza del radio. Radioulnar distal: la cabeza de la ulna con la escotadura ulnar del radio.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The proximal end of the ulna articulates with the trochlea of the humerus.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “this area articulates with the head of the radius”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The head articulates with the ulnar notch of the radius to form the distal radio-ulnar joint.”
- **Inserciones musculares.** Olécranon: tríceps braquial y cabeza ulnar del flexor ulnar del carpo. Tuberosidad de la ulna: braquial. Proceso coronoides: una de las dos cabezas del pronador redondo. Ulna y membrana interósea: flexor profundo de los dedos. Parte distal de la cara anterior: pronador cuadrado.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The triceps brachii muscle attaches to its superior surface.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “the ulnar head originates from the olecranon of the ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “It is where the brachialis muscle attaches.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “the other from the coronoid process of the ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “Originates from the ulna and associated interosseous membrane.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “site of attachment for the pronator quadratus muscle distally”
- **Relación.** Por su escotadura radial se articula con la cabeza del radio.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “this area articulates with the head of the radius”

### Músculo pronador redondo (`pronador-redondo`)

*Musculus pronator teres* · TA2 n.º 2478, 2479, 2480

- **Descripción.** Músculo de la capa superficial del compartimento anterior del antebrazo, con dos orígenes. Su borde lateral forma el borde medial de la fosa cubital.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “Superficial – flexor carpi ulnaris, palmaris longus, flexor carpi radialis, pronator teres.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “It has two origins, one from the medial epicondyle”

- **Origen.** Epicóndilo medial del húmero (por el tendón común de los flexores superficiales) y proceso coronoides de la ulna.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “one from the medial epicondyle, and the other from the coronoid process of the ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “They all originate from a common tendon, which arises from the medial epicondyle of the humerus”
- **Inserción.** Mitad de la diáfisis del radio, por su cara lateral.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “It attaches laterally to the mid-shaft of the radius”
- **Acción.** Prona el antebrazo.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “Pronation of the forearm.”
- **Inervación.** Nervio mediano.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “The pronator teres and pronator quadratus are the muscles responsible for pronation of the forearm. Both…”
- **Irrigación.** Arterias ulnar y radial (las del compartimento anterior del antebrazo).
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “They receive arterial supply from the ulnar artery and radial artery.”
- **Relación.** El nervio mediano pasa entre sus dos cabezas.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Median Nerve»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/nerves/median-nerve/): “the nerve travels between the two heads of pronator teres”
- **Relación.** Se inserta en la rugosidad de la mitad de la cara lateral del radio.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “In the middle of the lateral surface, there is a small roughening for the attachment of…”

### Músculo flexor superficial de los dedos (`flexor-superficial-dedos`)

*Musculus flexor superficialis digitorum* · TA2 n.º 2486, 2487, 2488

- **Descripción.** Único músculo de la capa intermedia del compartimento anterior del antebrazo: en la mayoría de las personas está entre las capas superficial y profunda. Es una buena referencia anatómica: el nervio mediano y la arteria ulnar pasan entre sus dos cabezas.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “The flexor digitorum superficialis is the only muscle of the intermediate compartment.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “in most individuals, it lies between the deep and superficial muscle layers”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “The muscle is a good anatomical landmark in the forearm – the median nerve and ulnar…”
- **Origen.** Una cabeza, del epicóndilo medial del húmero; la otra, del radio.

## Lección

- **Radio y ulna.** Los dos huesos del antebrazo. La ulna, medial, es el hueso estable; el radio, lateral, gira a su alrededor en las articulaciones radioulnares proximal y distal.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “The ulna acts as the stabilising bone, with the radius pivoting to produce movement.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “It lies laterally and parallel to ulna”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Ulna»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/ulna/): “It lies medially and parallel to the radius”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Radius»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/bones/radius/): “The radius pivots around the ulna to produce movement at the proximal and distal radio-ulnar joints.”
- **Capa superficial.** Cuatro músculos que nacen de un tendón común en el epicóndilo medial del húmero: flexor ulnar del carpo, palmar largo, flexor radial del carpo y pronador redondo. Los inerva el nervio mediano, salvo el flexor ulnar del carpo, que inerva el ulnar.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “They all originate from a common tendon, which arises from the medial epicondyle of the humerus”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “They are mostly innervated by the median nerve (except for the flexor carpi ulnaris”
- **Capa intermedia.** Un solo músculo, el flexor superficial de los dedos. Sus cuatro tendones llegan a la falange media de los dedos, y entre sus dos cabezas pasan el nervio mediano y la arteria ulnar.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “The flexor digitorum superficialis is the only muscle of the intermediate compartment.”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “attach to the base of the middle phalanx of the four digits”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “the median nerve and ulnar artery pass between its two heads”
- **Capa profunda.** Flexor profundo de los dedos, flexor largo del pulgar y pronador cuadrado. Los inerva el nervio interóseo anterior, rama del mediano, salvo la mitad medial del flexor profundo, que inerva el ulnar.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Muscles in the Anterior Compartment of the Forearm»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/muscles/anterior-forearm/): “There are three muscles in the deep anterior forearm”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «The Median Nerve»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/nerves/median-nerve/): “Deep layer – flexor pollicis longus, pronator quadratus, and the lateral half of the flexor digitorum…”
- **Arterias radial y ulnar.** La braquial se divide en la fosa cubital en radial y ulnar. La radial irriga la parte posterolateral del antebrazo y la ulnar, la anteromedial; en la mano se unen en los arcos palmares superficial y profundo.
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Arterial Supply to the Upper Limb»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/vessels/arteries/): “The radial and ulnar arteries are formed by the bifurcation of the brachial artery within the…”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Arterial Supply to the Upper Limb»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/vessels/arteries/): “supplies the posterolateral aspect of the forearm”
  - [TeachMeAnatomy, «Arterial Supply to the Upper Limb»](https://teachmeanatomy.info/upper-limb/vessels/arteries/): “supplies the anteromedial aspect of the forearm”
